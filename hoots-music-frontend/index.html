                    What we collect

                    Your name and email (to run your account), the songs you play and search for (to power recommendations), and basic device/session info (to keep you logged in and show login history).

                    To run playback, build your "Recommended for you" and "Trending" feeds, save your playlists, and show ads at the frequency you've configured — never sold to third parties.

                    Google (if you sign in with Google) and YouTube (audio is sourced from YouTube — see our Terms for details). We don't use third-party ad-tracking networks unless one is explicitly configured by the app owner.
